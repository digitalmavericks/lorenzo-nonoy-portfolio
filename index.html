How I work

The offer is not the system.

SYS / 01

Data that survives.

UTMs, landing-page URL, join and replay links, and engagement scoring stay intact from first click through close. If sales cannot see how the lead got there, the funnel is theater.

SYS / 02

Watch behavior, not just “showed up.”

Time-based attendance zones and CTA tags so follow-up matches what someone actually watched. No-show recovery is a path, not a hope.

SYS / 03

Mail that lands.

Sending-domain DNS and DMARC, mailbox warmup, and immediate-send automations for openers and clickers. Campaigns do not wait on a broken inbox.

SYS / 04

AI that has a job.

Conversation AI and voice with tags, pipelines, payment links, and objection logging. The bot is a closer’s assistant, not a chatbot on a landing page.
